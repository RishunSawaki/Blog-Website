## Why keep locales separate

Each language can evolve independently without mixing translation concerns into the page logic.

## Practical payoff

The route prefix is enough to swap labels, metadata, and posts without adding complexity to the editor workflow.

## Keep it simple

When the content is personal, a filesystem-first model is often the lowest-friction choice.
